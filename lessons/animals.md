# Lesson: Animals and Abilities

Target:
Grade 6 beginner English.

Objectives:
- Learn common animal vocabulary.
- Understand "can" and "cannot".
- Produce sentences such as:
  - The eagle can fly.
  - The fish can swim.

Teaching rules:
- Keep explanations short.
- Prefer interaction over lectures.
- Ask one question at a time.
- Do not immediately reveal answers.
- Give simple Vietnamese explanation if the student is confused.
- After an activity, react to the student's result.
